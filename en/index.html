The EDEN Residence

A high-end residential building with a ground floor and two upper floors in Chotrana 1, Soukra. 24 apartments across two blocks: 7 one-bedroom (S+1), 10 two-bedroom (S+2) and 7 three-bedroom (S+3) units — some with private gardens and swimming pools on the ground floor.

The residence is guarded 24/7, with a caretaker's lodge and a homeowners-association office on the ground floor. Remote-controlled underground parking with 27 car spaces and 18 storage cellars. Two lifts serve both blocks directly from the basement.

  • Split air conditioning
  • Fitted kitchens
  • Central heating
  • Electric shutters
  • Two lifts
  • Green areas
  • Underground parking
  • Satellite TV
