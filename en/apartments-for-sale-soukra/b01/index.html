Apartment B01 — two-bedroom (S+2), 124.22 m², Ground floor block B, EDEN Residence Soukra

Floor plan

A ground-floor two-bedroom extended by a real garden

Apartment B01 of the EDEN Residence is located on the ground floor of block B, with a total area of 124.22 m² (105.44 m² of living space).

It offers a day area and a night area separated by a sliding door.

Day area: a large living room of nearly 25 m² opening onto a terrace and a garden of about 38 m², a beautiful kitchen, a laundry room and a guest toilet near the entrance.

Night area: a bedroom with a walk-in wardrobe, a bathroom, and a master suite with its own bathroom and a large walk-in wardrobe.

The apartment enjoys all the amenities of the residence, which is guarded 24/7, with remote-controlled underground parking (27 car spaces, 18 storage cellars). Two lifts serve the ground floor and both blocks directly from the basement.
